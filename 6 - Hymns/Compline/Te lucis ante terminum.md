### Te lucis ante terminum
1. To Thee, before the close of day, / Creator of the world, we pray / That with Thy wonted favor, Thou / Wouldst be our Guard and Keeper now. 
2. From all ill dreams defend our eyes, / From nightly fears and fantasies: / Tread under foot our ghostly foe, / That no pollution we may know. 
3. O Father, that we ask be done / Through Jesus Christ, Thine only Son, / Who, with the Holy Ghost and Thee, / Shall live and reign eternally. Amen.
(Before 8th c., trans. by J. M. Neale.)
